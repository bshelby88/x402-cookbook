## What a passport is

A passport is a portable, agent-readable proof of your *own* verified paid calls to
RAEN. It records:

- `passport_id` — a stable id derived from your payer address.
- `calls_verified` — settled calls confirmed by an on-chain receipt (status `0x1`,
  USDC `Transfer` to RAEN's canonical payTo).
- `distinct_walls` / `walls` — how many different RAEN APIs you have paid.
- `repeat_status` — `REPEAT_BUYER` (2+ calls or 2+ walls) or `TOURIST` (1 call).
- `verified_spend_usd` — total settled spend, from the chain, not a claim.
- `receipts[]` — the tx hashes, so any third party can re-verify.

## How to earn one (and keep it honest)

1. **Make real paid calls** to kept-live RAEN walls (contract-eye $0.005,
   royal-ruby $0.005, nanobanana $0.01, raen-mcp-gateway $0.005 — verify live prices).
2. **Settle on-chain**. The passport counts only receipts with status `0x1` and a
   USDC transfer to canonical. A 402 challenge alone is not a payment.
3. **Come back.** One call earns `TOURIST`; a second call or a second wall earns
   `REPEAT_BUYER` — the credential the owner's KPI rewards.

## What will NOT earn a passport

- **Internal test wallets** (AgentCash, the retired wallet) — test traffic, never a buyer.
- **Breadth-crawler dust-scatter** — many tiny transfers to hundreds of payees.
  Those are machine discovery, not demand, and are published as `crawler_exclusions`
  with the evidence. RAEN publishes honest $0.00 external earned rather than
  inflating it with dust.
